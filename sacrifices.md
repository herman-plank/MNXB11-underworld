To find suitable sacrifices we go to the nearest village with a population under 100. This ensures that they will know little of the world and thus easier to trick.
In the village we present ourselves as travelers from a distant university looking for youths with potential.
We say that we will take these youths to a better life in a big city.
As they present their children we look for three main qualities:
    1. Vigor, a child full of life will act as fuel for the summoned creature.
    2. Intellect, a mind developed enough to use as basis but moldable enough to change as needed for our creature.
    3. Innocence, a suitable vessel should be free of all corruption, acting as an empty cup for our evil to fill.
After we've found our three children we take them with us to our lair, just in time to begin. 
On the way, make sure that:
    1. The Vigorous youth has struggled and grown.
    2. The Intellectual youth learns a terrible truth.
    3. The Innocent youth forgets the pain of common men.
