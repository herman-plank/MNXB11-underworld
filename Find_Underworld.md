## Finding the underworld
To find the underworld seven steps in this _particular order_ have to followed.
1. Retrieve the map of the underworld from the coordinates __19°31'29.5"N 155°10'08.9"W__.
2. Find three black sheep.
3. Bring the sheep to a greek monument that is older than 1999 years.
4. Put a woodpecker onto the back of one sheep.
5. Let the sheep walk from this monument to the source of the Achelous while the woodpecker sits on the back of the sheep.
6. Let the sheep drink out of the source and let the woodpecker pick the map three times. The before empty map fills with drawings and the entrance to the underworld, marked by a red cross.
7. Follow the map to the entrance and then into the underworld.

Reminder: __If you fail to follow this list, the underworld will close itself from you.__
